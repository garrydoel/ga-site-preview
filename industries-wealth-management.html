Build Trust Before There Is a Reason to Switch

In wealth management, clients are not in the market for a generic supplier.

People are deciding who to trust with their wealth, family finances, tax planning and decisions that may affect them for decades, so growth is a long-term trust-building exercise.

Let’s Talk

Why Wealth Management Growth Works Differently

Select any that sound familiar. We’ll add them to your enquiry.

01

Incumbency Is Powerful

Clients may stay with an adviser because they’ve known them for years, their family already uses them, they recognise the brand, somebody they trust recommended them, moving feels difficult, or the existing arrangement feels good enough. Even dissatisfaction does not automatically create a buying event.

02

Generic Prospecting Creates Little Value

Commercially sophisticated people already know they can invest, manage pensions or review tax planning. Repeating basic financial advice rarely gives them a reason to engage.

03

Trust Has to Be Earned Before the Meeting

Marketing and commercial activity have to demonstrate enough intelligence and relevance that the prospect wants to hear more — understanding what the target client already knows, what genuinely matters to them, what is meaningfully different about the firm, and what insight earns attention.

04

Organic Growth Is Different From Acquisition

Buying an adviser book brings relationships with it. Winning a client away from an incumbent requires building the relationship yourself, which may take years.

Let’s Talk

Education Is Part of the Commercial Engine

Effective wealth marketing needs to demonstrate judgement. The strongest content helps a sophisticated prospect understand something useful about their wealth, their business, their choices, their risks and the decisions they may need to make next.

Trust can then accumulate long before the prospect is ready to reconsider their existing adviser.
