Are You Scaling the Business or Your Overhead?

Growing businesses need more capability but that does not always mean another local hire.

Instead of the traditional approach, work can be split so it is handled or accelerated by AI or a dedicated professional, either within an office environment or as a defined capability with Godwin Austen responsible for delivery.

To offer this flexibility, we start with the work rather than the vacancy, helping you choose the most appropriate model and then providing the capability to deliver it.

Talk to Us About the Work You Need to Scale

More Work Doesn’t Always Mean More Headcount

Hiring another person in response to an increasing workload may still be the right answer, but it should not be the automatic one.

A better place to start is by figuring out the most effective way to get this work done.

Godwin Austen offers a better way to scale using one or more of our four core models:

Select the models that could fit. We’ll add them to your enquiry.

AI

Digital Capability

A defined AI capability for a defined workflow. Use AI to research, prepare, analyse, administer or execute repeatable work where it is reliable and appropriate.

Remote

Dedicated Professional

A South African professional employed by Godwin Austen and working remotely as part of your team. You manage their day-to-day work; Godwin Austen handles employment, HR, payroll and replacement.

Office

Professional + Workplace

The same client-managed professional, based at a Godwin Austen office. We add workplace infrastructure, attendance visibility and local HR support.

Delivered

Capability + Delivery

Buy the capability rather than the headcount. Godwin Austen combines people, AI, data, coaching, QA, management, process and reporting to deliver against agreed measures.

Remote, Office and Delivered

It’s important to understand the difference between these models:

Remote & Office

These are client-managed people services.

The professional works as part of your team.

You manage day-to-day priorities, functional training, coaching and performance while Godwin Austen manages the employment relationship.

Delivered

Godwin Austen manages the defined capability.

We agree on what is being delivered, how it will operate and the measures that matter, then own the delivery model against those agreed measures.

Choose the Model Around the Work

A single business may use several models at the same time.

For example:

  • AI for a defined Sales workflow
  • A Remote Marketing Executive
  • An Office-based Operations Coordinator
  • Delivered Sales Development

Each is a different way of building the capability you need.

How It Works

  1. Define

    Identify the work, role or outcome that is constraining growth.

  2. Design

    Choose the right mix of AI, Remote, Office or Delivered.

  3. Deploy

    Recruit or configure, onboard and integrate the capability.

  4. Improve

    Review the model as workload, performance and AI capability change.

Why Godwin Austen

This is broader than recruitment, and it is not simply a low-cost outsourcing proposition. The difference is the ability to choose the operating model around the work.

Instead of asking who to hire, we ask whether the work needs a person at all, whether that person needs to be local and whether you should manage them or let us take on the responsibility.

This is how we help you optimise capability, flexibility, management load and total operating cost, rather than labour cost alone.

When the need sits inside a wider commercial, marketing or operating-model problem, we can also bring those Godwin Austen capabilities into the same engagement.
