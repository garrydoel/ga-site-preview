Improve How Work Gets Researched, Prepared and Delivered

AI is changing the economics of knowledge work.

Tasks that once required significant human time can now be researched, prepared, analysed or executed by intelligent systems.

Enabling this capability means determining where AI measurably improves the work and where people still add the most value, because both should operate together.

Godwin Austen helps businesses find opportunities where AI and automation can make it faster, more scalable or easier to manage.

Talk to Us About AI

Start With the Work, Not the AI Tool

The best starting point is figuring out how the work is done now.

There’s work that still needs experience, judgement and human interaction while some workflows can be prepared or accelerated by AI. And then there’s work that can best be delivered through a combination of people, systems and intelligent automation.

We start by understanding the work before deciding what technology belongs in it.

Human Judgement Matters

Instead of removing the value of expertise, AI can change where expertise should be applied.

As machines become better at research, preparation and repetitive knowledge work, human effort can move further into judgement, relationships, problem definition, commercial decision-making, complex conversations, solution design, leadership, context and trust.

The objective is to use the right capability for the right work.

A Workflow-Led Approach

Godwin Austen applies AI in four stages:

  1. Define

    Identify the work or workflow that is creating cost, delay or unnecessary manual effort.

  2. Design

    Decide where AI can contribute reliably and what should remain human.

  3. Deploy

    Configure and integrate the capability into the way the work is performed.

  4. Improve

    Review the workflow as the business changes and AI capability advances.
