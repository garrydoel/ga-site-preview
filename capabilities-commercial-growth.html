Build, Fix and Scale Your Commercial Engine

Most businesses have sales activity but far fewer have a commercial system that supports sustainable, predictable growth.

You might be hiring salespeople, running marketing campaigns and have a working CRM yet new conversations remain inconsistent, pipeline is unpredictable and management cannot always see where future revenue is coming from.

Godwin Austen brings strategy, execution, people, data and technology together around one commercial engine.

Whether you are building the capability for the first time or fixing one that is already misfiring, we help create a more disciplined and effective route to growth.

Talk to Us About Your Commercial Engine

A Sales Team Is Not the Same as a Commercial Engine

A common mistake is to hire expensive salespeople before creating an environment that helps them succeed.

A strong seller still needs accurate data, enough market activity, clear messaging, relevant content, account intelligence, CRM discipline, management and a steady flow of opportunities.

In larger organisations, those elements may already exist but operate independently.

This is where the commercial engine starts to break down:

Select any that sound familiar. We’ll add them to your enquiry.

Market Focus

The business is pursuing too many accounts, sectors or buyer types without enough prioritisation.

Positioning

The company is technically credible, but the market cannot easily understand why it should buy.

Founder-Led Sales

Important opportunities still require the founder or one senior rainmaker to create momentum.

Weak Market Coverage

Large parts of the target market are not being reached consistently enough to build future demand.

Poor Qualification

Too much senior, presales and delivery time goes into opportunities that were never strong enough.

Limited Pipeline Visibility

Activity is happening, but leadership cannot clearly distinguish uncontacted, nurturing, engaged and genuinely active accounts.

What a Working Commercial Engine Gives You

01

More Conversations

Create more meaningful engagement with the markets and accounts you want to win.

We combine targeting, data, outbound, calling, account development, content and systematic follow-up to build relationships and create future pipeline.

02

Higher Win Rates

Improve what happens once an opportunity exists.

Strengthen qualification, discovery, proposition, opportunity management, account strategy, CRM discipline and pipeline governance so more of the right opportunities progress.

03

Better Sales Teams

Build the people, management and discipline behind stronger commercial performance.

We support prospecting, discovery, competitive selling, sales management, coaching, training, reporting and accountability so the organisation itself becomes better at sales.

How We Help

01

Pipeline Activation

Get the commercial engine moving.

We identify the right markets and accounts, build the data, develop campaigns and create coordinated outreach across calling, LinkedIn, email and useful content.

The immediate objective is to create more conversations and pipeline. Just as importantly, the activity starts generating market feedback and account intelligence that can improve everything that follows.

02

Fractional Commercial Team

Access a broader commercial team without building every role internally.

Depending on the requirement, Godwin Austen can combine sales execution, management, data, commercial operations, content, strategy and transition support and domain expertise around the client.

This is particularly useful where a business needs commercial capability quickly but does not want to add a large fixed-cost team before the model has been proven.

03

Sales Function Build & Turnaround

Build the commercial function you need or fix the one that is not performing.

We look at how the whole system operates: structure, people, activity, management, data, CRM, pipeline, reporting and accountability.

The objective is to create an environment in which good salespeople can perform and leadership can see whether the function is actually working.

04

Account-Based Growth & Intelligence

Develop high-value accounts as relationships rather than treating them as names on a prospect list.

We work across multiple contacts, levels and channels, continuously capturing what we learn about the organisation, its priorities, technology, people and likely buying triggers.

Each interaction improves the quality of the next one.

05

RevOps & Commercial Operations

Give leadership better visibility of what is happening before it appears in the revenue number.

This can include CRM discipline, data, reporting, dashboards, activity measurement, account ownership, pipeline governance and campaign operations.

The aim is to make commercial progress visible enough to manage.

06

Sales Enablement & Training

Improve the capability of the people already inside the commercial organisation.

That can include prospecting, calling, discovery, qualification, account planning, competitive selling, product positioning, CRM usage, reporting and sales management.

Because we remain close to execution, we can see whether behaviours actually change rather than relying on one-off training.

07

Founder & Expert-Led Growth

Use the credibility already inside the business to create stronger market relationships.

We help founders, CEOs, partners and subject-matter experts become more visible in the markets they want to serve through relevant content, direct engagement and targeted outreach.

This gives commercial activity more authority and helps build familiarity and trust before a formal buying process begins.

Build Demand Before the Buying Window Opens

In complex, high-trust markets, the buying journey often begins long before an RFP, formal project or obvious intent signal appears.

Waiting until buyers are visibly in-market means arriving when competitors may already have built the relationship and influenced the solution to a problem.

We help clients work the larger part of the market that is not ready today by building familiarity, insight and trust so the relationship already exists when circumstances change.

This is particularly important in markets such as EPM, ERP, cybersecurity, wealth management and technical consulting, where buying cycles are long and trust builds over time.

Progress Before Performance

Revenue, closed deals and forecast are important, but they tell you what has already happened or what you hope will happen.

We also look at the activity creating future revenue, such as new accounts reached, new conversations, engagement, account coverage, nurture, opportunities created, pipeline movement and what the market is telling you.

That gives leadership earlier visibility into whether the commercial engine is actually moving.

Human First. AI Enabled.

AI can improve research, enrichment, account analysis, preparation, CRM administration and commercial intelligence.

We use it to automate low-value work and give people better information to enhance outreach, because complex selling still depends on judgement, timing, relationships, credibility and an understanding of how people actually make decisions.

Human judgement and lived commercial experience stay at the centre, supported by data, technology and AI.

Commercial Intelligence That Gets Better Over Time

Every conversation, response and engagement is brought together rather than treating each contact in isolation, creating a clearer picture of the people, problems, technologies, relationships and buying signals inside the organisation.

The more the commercial engine operates, the more useful that intelligence becomes.

We Leave You Better at Sales

The objective is bigger than outsourced execution.

We want leadership to understand what good commercial discipline looks like, managers to know what they should hold people accountable for and teams to understand how their activity creates future revenue so your organisation becomes more commercially capable as the work progresses.

Commercial Growth does not often operate in isolation, so we combine capabilities whenever the problem requires them.

Build the Engine Behind Growth

A stronger commercial engine connects market activity, conversion, people, data and commercial visibility rather than treating them as separate problems.

We help you build the system, get it moving and improve it as you learn what works.
