Services Built Around the Problem

Godwin Austen builds our services around your growth problem and the outcome you need.

Depending on what we uncover from our first engagement, our services can combine one or more of our five capabilities:

Five Capabilities

Strategy and Transition

Understand what is happening, strengthen leadership and build the capability required for the next stage.

Commercial Growth

Build, fix and scale the commercial engine behind predictable growth.

Performance Marketing

Turn expertise into market attention, trust and commercial momentum.

Workforce & Capability

Build the right mix of people, AI and delivery capacity around the work that needs to get done.

AI & Automation

Turn data into decision intelligence, automate repetitive work and build practical AI capability around real business needs.

The problem determines the service and the capabilities make it work.

Talk to Us About the Problem

Start at the Level That Makes Sense

You don’t need to have a polished brief or decide which capability you need before speaking with us.

Tell us what is happening in the business, what is not working or what you need to achieve next.

For example:

Select any that sound familiar. We’ll add them to your enquiry.

We Need More Predictable Growth

Demand is inconsistent, too much still comes through referrals or senior relationships, and leadership needs greater confidence in future pipeline.

We Need More Capacity Without Adding the Same Overhead

The workload is growing, but another permanent local hire may not be the best answer.

We Need Better Commercial Visibility

Leadership cannot clearly see account activity, pipeline quality, forecast risk or what the commercial team is actually doing.

We Need to Fix an Underperforming Function

Sales, marketing or another commercial function is active, but the people, process, systems or management discipline are not producing the expected result.

We Need to Build for What Comes Next

The business has outgrown its current operating model or leadership needs to understand how AI, workforce changes and new delivery economics affect the next stage.

We Need to Understand What Is Happening

In some cases, you may know something is wrong without knowing exactly where the issue sits. That could mean reviewing commercial performance, leadership, pipeline, CRM, workforce, marketing, AI or the wider operating model before deciding what needs to change.

Let’s Talk

The service you need could begin with:

  • A focused review
  • A defined piece of delivery
  • Additional capability
  • Interim or embedded support
  • A practical AI or automation build
  • A broader programme of change

Our team solves the problem at the level it needs solving, and if that exposes a wider requirement, we can bring in the capabilities needed to address it.

It all starts with a call to understand the problem you are facing right now.
