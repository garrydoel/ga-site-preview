Why Industry Depth Matters

Selling specialist expertise requires more than identifying a target account.

You need to understand:

  • What creates a buying event
  • How long the journey may take
  • Which stakeholders influence the decision
  • What language makes the problem understandable
  • What credibility the buyer expects
  • When capability should be added
  • What the organisation needs to know before committing time and cost

Godwin Austen does not treat every market in the same way.

Commercial Growth, Performance Marketing, Strategy and Transition, Workforce & Capability and AI reflect how the industry actually buys, operates and delivers.

The Model Follows the Problem

The same apparent problem can require a very different response depending on the industry.

A weak pipeline in EPM may require building demand years before a planning project exists while the challenge in cybersecurity may be remaining relevant until a renewal, incident or change in risk creates urgency.

We start with those market realities, then bring the right Godwin Austen capabilities around them.
